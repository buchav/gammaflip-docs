## What is the GammaFlip API?

The GammaFlip API gives you programmatic access to the same Gamma Exposure (GEX)
data that powers [gammaflip.io](https://gammaflip.io), for crypto options
(Bybit, Deribit, OKX, Derive) and US options (SPX, QQQ, SPY and more). Build
trading bots or integrate GEX signals into your research and workflow.

## Access and limits

API access is a plan entitlement. The 14-day trial includes it (50 requests/day);
other plans with API access get 500 requests/day and up to 5 keys. Each plan
covers the `crypto` market, the `tradfi` market, or both. Discovery endpoints and
[`/me`](/api-reference/me) are free and not counted.
See [Rate limits](/rate-limits) and [Authentication](/authentication), or
[get access](https://gammaflip.io/subscribe).

## What you can do

- Get the key levels (flip, regime, walls, totals) for a symbol in one small call
- Query GEX data by strike, by expiration, or aggregated across venues
- List available exchanges, coins, and expiration dates
- Use crypto venues (Bybit, Deribit, OKX, Derive) separately, combined, or as a subset like `deribit+derive`
- Query US tickers such as `SPX` and `QQQ` under the `tradfi` exchange
- Know how fresh every response is: `as_of`, `oi_as_of` and more in `meta`
- Read the stored history: levels over time, per-strike snapshots and daily CSV files for backtests. See [Historical data](/history)

See [Markets and exchanges](/markets-and-exchanges) and the [data dictionary](/data-dictionary).
